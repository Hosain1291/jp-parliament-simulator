Questions and party positions simplify the main issues of each election and may differ from actual party lines and votes. 1928–1993 used multi-member districts (1946: limited-vote large districts) with no proportional tier; from 1996 the PR tier is computed nationally instead of in 11 blocks. Independents are counted as one group. Before the war and in 1946 the Emperor appointed the Prime Minister, so the nomination vote shown is hypothetical. Party colours follow Wikipedia; a few unverified ones (prewar and occupation-era parties) are approximations. The prefecture map uses jp.json (simplemaps).
